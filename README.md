### Aligned with 2026 MEB Curriculum & LGS (High School Entrance Exam) Standards

## 📌 Project Overview
This study pack is crafted to provide 8th-grade students with high-yield revision, new-generation question practice, and open-ended written exam preparation for the **1st Semester 1st Written Exam**.

### 🎯 MEB Curriculum Learning Outcomes (Kazanımlar)
- **E8.1.L1 / E8.1.SI1 / E8.1.W1**: 
  - Accepting and refusing invitations, making apologies, and giving explanations/reasons.
  - Identifying personal traits of true friends and discussing interpersonal relationships.
- **E8.2.L1 / E8.2.SI1 / E8.2.R1 / E8.2.W1**:
  - Expressing personal preferences (likes, dislikes, interests) using *keen on, crazy about, fond of, can't stand, prefer*.
  - Talking about daily routines, frequency of activities using adverbs of frequency (*always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never*).
  - Interpreting visual data (survey charts, preference tables, schedules).

## 💡 Exam Tips for Students
- **Watch out for Negatives**: Questions with `NOT correct`, `NO information`, or `CANNOT be answered` require double-checking every single choice.
- **Synonyms & Collocations**: 
  - *count on = rely on = trust*
  - *back up = support*
  - *have a lot in common = share similar interests*
  - *can't stand = unbearable = hate*
- **Visual & Chart Reading**: Focus on percentage leaders, least preferred items, and compare columns carefully.
