#### Exercise 2: Phrasal Verbs & Expressions Fill-in-the-Blanks
1. **back up:** *"A true friend should always back up you when you face difficulties..."*  
   *Note: In LGS questions, "back up" is almost always paraphrased as "support" or "help someone when in need."*
2. **keep a secret:** *"...because I know she will always keep a secret and never tell anyone."*  
   *Note: Opposite of "share secrets with others" or "tell lies."*
3. **get on well with:** *"...they still get on well with each other because they respect each other's opinions."*  
   *Note: Means having a smooth, friendly relationship (iyi geçinmek).*
4. **tells lies:** *"An honest person never tells lies; they always tell what really happened."*
5. **count on:** *"You can always count on Robert; he is the most reliable boy in our class."*  
   *Note: Synonyms that frequently appear in questions include "rely on", "depend on", and "trust".*
6. **have a lot in common:** *"Since Aylin and I both love playing tennis, camping... we have a lot in common."*  
   *Note: Paraphrased as "have similar interests/hobbies."*  
*(Extra word provided in box: **drift apart** – to slowly become less friendly over time).*

### SECTION B: UNIT 2 – TEEN LIFE

#### Exercise 4: Preferences & Opinions Fill-in-the-Blanks
1. **keen on:** *"...she is really keen on classical music."* (Synonym of *fond of / crazy about / interested in*).
2. **can't stand / unbearable:** *"I can't stand heavy metal music... I find it completely unbearable."* (Can't stand = tahammül edememek; Unbearable = katlanılmaz).
3. **prefer ... to ...:** *"Most teenagers prefer listening to music on digital platforms to buying audio CDs..."* (Note grammar: *Prefer [doing X] to [doing Y]*).
4. **ridiculous:** *"...because he thinks they look ridiculous and funny."* (Gülünç, anlamsız).
5. **impressive:** *"The guitarist's solo performance... was truly impressive; everyone applauded..."* (Etkileyici).
6. **crazy about:** *"Hande is crazy about skating; she goes to the skatepark almost every single day."*
7. **trendy:** *"Following fashion blogs helps teens learn about the most trendy clothes..."* (Modaya uygun, popüler).

#### Exercise 5: Frequency Adverbs & Daily Routines
**A. Percentage Equivalents:**
- **100%:** always
- **80% - 90%:** usually
- **50% - 70%:** often
- **20% - 30%:** sometimes
- **5% - 10%:** rarely / seldom
- **0%:** never

**B. Sentence Completions:**
1. **never:** Kerem hates waking up early on Sundays. He (0%) **never** gets out of bed before 10:30 AM.
2. **always:** Selin loves books. She (100%) **always** reads at least twenty pages before going to sleep.
3. **often:** Mete plays basketball four days a week. He (70%) **often** trains with his school team after classes.
4. **rarely / seldom:** Cemal dislikes fast food. He (5%) **rarely** (or **seldom**) eats hamburgers or French fries.

## 🔍 PART 2: READING & COMPREHENSION (LGS QUESTIONS DETAILED ANALYSIS)

### QUESTION 1
> **Question Stem:** According to the conversation above, who refuses the invitation by giving an excuse?

* **Correct Answer:** **B) Chloe**
* **In-Depth Rationale:**
  - In LGS exams, the distinction between a **bare refusal / personal dislike** and an **excuse** is tested heavily.
  - Chloe says: *"I'd love to be there with you, but my cousin is getting married on Saturday afternoon, so I have to attend the wedding."* She gives an actual, concrete social reason preventing her from attending.
* **Why the Distractors are Incorrect:**
  - **A) Brian:** He enthusiastically accepts the invitation (*"That sounds fantastic... Count me in!"*).
  - **C) Denis:** A classic LGS trap distractor. Denis refuses (*"Thanks for inviting me, Mark. However, I can't stand outdoor sports..."*), but he only gives his **personal negative attitude / dislike**, NOT an excuse. In the MEB curriculum, saying "I hate cycling" is an expression of preference, not an apology/excuse (mazeret).
  - **D) Emma:** She expresses interest and asks for clarification/more information about transportation (*"Will there be a school bus...?"*); she does not refuse.

### QUESTION 2
> **Question Stem:** Which of the following questions DOES NOT have an answer in the invitation card?

* **Correct Answer:** **C) How will the participants travel from their school to Pine Forest?**
* **In-Depth Rationale:**
  - Scanning the poster shows:
    - Date: Saturday, October 24, 2026.
    - Time: 09:30 AM – 05:00 PM.
    - Location: Pine Forest National Park.
    - Fee: $15 per person.
    - Mandatory items: Bring your own bicycle and helmet.
    - Deadline: October 20, 2026.
  - The poster contains NO information about bus transit, meeting points at the school, or how participants get to Pine Forest. This exact omission triggers Emma’s question in the chat.
* **Why the Distractors are Incorrect:**
  - **A:** Answered on the poster: *"Please inform the organizer until October 20, 2026."*
  - **B:** Answered on the poster: *"Don't forget to bring your own bicycle and helmet!"*
  - **D:** Answered on the poster: *"Fee: $15 per person (Includes lunch and hot drinks)"*.

### QUESTION 3
> **Question Stem:** According to the research results and tables above, which of the following statements is CORRECT?

* **Correct Answer:** **B) Rap music is equally popular among both teenage boys and teenage girls.**
* **In-Depth Rationale:**
  - In Table 2 (Music Preferences):
    - Rap music among Boys = **30%**
    - Rap music among Girls = **30%**
    - Therefore, Rap is enjoyed in equal proportions (*equally popular*) by both genders.
* **Why the Distractors are Incorrect:**
  - **A:** Table 1 shows that reading books & visiting exhibitions is 10% for boys and 30% for girls. It is three times more popular among girls, making option A factually reversed.
  - **C:** Table 1 shows that only 18% of girls prefer outdoor sports. "More than half" means over 50%, so 18% is completely false.
  - **D:** Table 2 shows that 38% of boys like Rock/Heavy Metal, while only 12% like classical music. Boys prefer rock over classical, not the other way around.

### QUESTION 4
> **Question Stem:** Read the comments below. Based on the survey data, which teenager's comment best reflects the general tendency of their peer group?

* **Correct Answer:** **B) Aylin (Girl): "I am crazy about pop music, and going to live concerts with my best friends is my favorite weekend activity."**
* **In-Depth Rationale:**
  - Cross-referencing the girls' data across both tables:
    - Highest musical preference for girls: **Pop music (42%)**.
    - Highest free-time activity for girls: **Attending live music concerts (38%)**.
    - Aylin's quote combines the top two choices of teenage girls, accurately representing the primary trend of her demographic.
* **Why the Distractors are Incorrect:**
  - **A (Jason):** Boys overwhelmingly prefer outdoor sports (46%, the highest rank) and dislike reading books/exhibitions (10%, the lowest). Jason’s quote claims the exact opposite.
  - **C (Kevin):** Boys prefer Rock/Heavy Metal most (38%) and Classical least (12%). Kevin's statement inverts this reality.
  - **D (Merve):** Video games are only played by 14% of girls (the lowest activity for girls), while 32% of boys play them. It is not the most popular activity for girls.

### QUESTION 5
> **Question Stem:** Read the dialogue below between Kevin and Sam... Which of the following completes Kevin's statement suitably according to the dialogue?

* **Correct Answer:** **C) we share the same opinion; classical music is much more relaxing and impressive**
* **In-Depth Rationale:**
  - Sam explicitly rejects loud/rock music (*"can't stand rock music... unbearable... prefer calmer tunes"*). Kevin starts his turn with *"I totally agree with you. In fact, ..."*. To logically continue, Kevin must affirm their shared taste and support calmer music.
* **Why the Distractors are Incorrect:**
  - **A:** If they both dislike rock music and prefer calmer tunes, they share common ground; stating they have *"nothing in common"* is a direct contradiction.
  - **B:** Saying *"I'm crazy about electric guitars and heavy metal concerts"* contradicts his agreement with Sam.
  - **D:** Recommending a rock festival contradicts the fact that both despise rock music.

## ✍️ PART 3: WRITING & PRODUCTION (MEB OPEN-ENDED CRITERIA & MODEL ANSWERS)

### MEB Official 10-Point Holistic Scoring Grid
| Criteria | Max Points | Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Task Completion** | **4 pts** | All prompt requirements (dates, excuses, reasons, comparison structures) are addressed. |
| **Vocabulary & Collocations** | **2 pts** | Accurate usage of target phrases (*count on, keen on, can't stand, prefer to, unbearable*). |
| **Grammar & Sentence Flow** | **2 pts** | Accurate syntax, correct tenses, subject-verb agreement, proper prepositions. |
| **Mechanics & Formatting** | **2 pts** | Appropriate salutation/closing, correct spelling, capitalization, punctuation, and word count compliance. |

---

### SCENARIO 1: REFUSING AN INVITATION WITH AN EXCUSE
* **Benchmark Model Answer (Full 10 Points):**
  ```text
  Hi Jack,
  Thanks a lot for the invitation to your PlayStation gaming night! I would really love to come, but I can't make it this Friday. My cousin is arriving from Ankara, and our family is having a welcome dinner together. I hope you have great fun. Can we hang out next weekend instead?
  Cheers,
  Mert
  ```
* **Key Rubric Checkpoints:**
  1. *Thanks given?* Yes (*"Thanks a lot for the invitation..."*).
  2. *Refusal present?* Yes (*"I can't make it this Friday."*).
  3. *Valid excuse provided?* Yes (*"My cousin is arriving from Ankara, and our family is having a welcome dinner..."*).
  4. *Polite closing / future contact?* Yes (*"Can we hang out next weekend instead?"*).
* **Common Student Mistakes:**
  - Writing "I don't like PlayStation" instead of an excuse.
  - Forgetting the greeting or thank-you phrase.
  - Writing incomplete sentence fragments.

---

### SCENARIO 2: WRITING AN INVITATION NOTE
* **Benchmark Model Answer (Full 10 Points):**
  ```text
  Dear Friends,
  We are organizing a Class Reunion Picnic to celebrate the start of the 8th grade!
  Date: Sunday, October 11, 2026
  Time: 11:30 AM - 03:30 PM
  Place: Pine Tree City Park
  Please let me know if you can join us until Friday, October 9. We hope to see everyone there!
  Elif (Class President)
  ```
* **Key Rubric Checkpoints:**
  1. *Event identified?* Yes (*Class Reunion Picnic*).
  2. *Date & time provided?* Yes (*Sunday, Oct 11, 2026, 11:30 AM - 03:30 PM*).
  3. *Location given?* Yes (*Pine Tree City Park*).
  4. *RSVP deadline given?* Yes (*until Friday, October 9*).
* **Common Student Mistakes:**
  - Omitting the deadline date ("Please let me know" without specifying a day).
  - Leaving out the start or end time.

### SCENARIO 3: EXPRESSING DAILY ROUTINES & PERSONAL PREFERENCES
* **Benchmark Model Answer (Full 10 Points):**
  ```text
  Dear Lucas,
  After school, I usually do my homework and then ride my bicycle in the park. I am crazy about playing volleyball because it is very energetic and healthy. However, I can't stand heavy metal music; I find it noisy and unbearable. In general, I prefer listening to pop songs to rock music.
  Write back soon,
  Arda
  ```
* **Key Rubric Checkpoints:**
  1. *Frequency adverb used?* Yes (*usually*).
  2. *Target expression used?* Yes (*crazy about playing volleyball* with reason: *energetic and healthy*).
  3. *Can't stand + reason?* Yes (*can't stand heavy metal music; noisy and unbearable*).
  4. *Prefer ... to ... structure?* Yes (*prefer listening to pop songs to rock music*).
* **Common Student Mistakes:**
  - Writing *prefer ... than ...* instead of the correct grammatical structure *prefer ... to ...*.
  - Forgetting the reason when stating a dislike.
  - Omitting the frequency adverb.
