## 6. READING & COMPREHENSION (LGS QUESTIONS DETAILED ANALYSIS)

### Question 1:
* **Correct Answer:** **B) Chloe**
* **Detailed Explanation:** 
  - Chloe clearly expresses that she wants to come (*"I'd love to be there with you..."*), but she provides a specific reason/excuse for not attending: her cousin is having a wedding on Saturday (*"...my cousin is getting married on Saturday afternoon, so I have to attend the wedding."*).
  - *Distractor Analysis:*
    - **A) Brian:** He accepts the invitation enthusiastically (*"Count me in!"*).
    - **C) Denis:** Denis refuses, BUT he DOES NOT give an excuse. He merely expresses his personal dislike/hatred of outdoor sports (*"I can't stand outdoor sports..."*). In LGS questions, refusing by stating personal dislike is NOT considered making an excuse. An excuse requires a circumstance/obligation (e.g., being ill, having an exam, family event).
    - **D) Emma:** Emma does not refuse; she is interested and asks for more details regarding transportation.

### Question 2:
* **Correct Answer:** **C) How will the participants travel from their school to Pine Forest?**
* **Detailed Explanation:**
  - In the invitation poster, we are informed about:
    - The fee: "$15 per person" (Answers D).
    - The equipment to bring: "bicycle and helmet" (Answers B).
    - The deadline to inform the organizer: "October 20, 2026" (Answers A).
  - However, there is NO information about transportation/travel method to Pine Forest. In fact, this is the exact missing information that Emma asks about in the WhatsApp chat (*"Will there be a school bus, or do we have to cycle all the way there?"*).
  - *Distractor Analysis:* Options A, B, and D all find explicit answers in the poster.

### Question 3:
* **Correct Answer:** **B) Rap music is equally popular among both teenage boys and teenage girls.**
* **Detailed Explanation:**
  - Looking at Table 2 (Music Preferences): Boys' preference for Rap is **30%**, and Girls' preference for Rap is **30%**. They are exactly equal (*equally popular*). Therefore, B is 100% correct.
  - *Distractor Analysis:*
    - **A) Incorrect:** Reading & visiting exhibitions is 10% for boys and 30% for girls. It is more popular among girls, not boys.
    - **C) Incorrect:** Doing outdoor sports is preferred by only 18% of girls. 18% is far less than "more than half" (which would need to be $>50\%$).
    - **D) Incorrect:** Boys prefer rock music at 38% and classical at only 12%. Hence, boys are keen on rock much more than classical.

### Question 4:
* **Correct Answer:** **B) Aylin (Girl): "I am crazy about pop music, and going to live concerts with my best friends is my favorite weekend activity."**
* **Detailed Explanation:**
  - Table 1 shows that going to live music concerts is the highest preferred activity for girls (38%). Table 2 shows that Pop music is the most popular music genre among girls (42%). Aylin's statement perfectly mirrors these leading percentages.
  - *Distractor Analysis:*
    - **A) Incorrect:** 46% of boys prefer outdoor sports like cycling and trekking (the #1 activity for boys), and only 10% read books. Jason's comment is the opposite of the general boy group trend.
    - **C) Incorrect:** Rock & heavy metal is the top music preference for boys (38%), while classical is only 12%. Kevin's comment goes against the majority trend.
    - **D) Incorrect:** Playing video games is preferred by only 14% of girls (it is their lowest interest after outdoor sports), so it is not the most popular activity for girls.

### Question 5:
* **Correct Answer:** **C) we share the same opinion; classical music is much more relaxing and impressive**
* **Detailed Explanation:**
  - Sam states that he can't stand rock music because it is too loud, noisy, and unbearable, and prefers calmer tunes. Kevin agrees (*"I totally agree with you"*). Therefore, Kevin must express a shared positive view towards calmer music and agree with Sam.
  - *Distractor Analysis:*
    - **A) Incorrect:** If they both dislike rock and prefer calm music, they *do* have common ground (*"nothing in common"* contradicts their agreement).
    - **B) Incorrect:** Being crazy about heavy metal contradicts Kevin's agreement with Sam.
    - **D) Incorrect:** Inviting him to a rock festival makes no sense right after agreeing that rock is unbearable.

### MODEL ANSWER – SCENARIO 3 (Lucas Reply - Routines & Preferences)
```text
Hi Lucas,
I usually do my homework and surf the Net after school. I am crazy about playing basketball because it is very energetic and fun. However, I can't stand heavy metal music; I find it unbearable and noisy. In my free time, I always prefer listening to pop music to rock music.
Take care,
[Student's Name]
```
* **Teacher Note:**
  - Frequency adverbs used: *usually*, *always*.
  - Expression used: *crazy about*.
  - Dislike with reason: *can't stand heavy metal music; I find it unbearable and noisy*.
  - Preference pattern: *prefer listening to pop music to rock music* (prefer X to Y).
